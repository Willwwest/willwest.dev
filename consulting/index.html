What can you bring me?

Start with a workflow someone on your team owns. That might be an analyst checking dataflows for errors or an operations team classifying and routing records. I work on the application and the connections, pipelines, and permissions it needs.

How do data access and security review work?

The first working session includes the people responsible for the data and security. We trace where the records sit, which permissions the workflow needs, and what blocked earlier attempts. Building against those constraints gives your reviewers working software to evaluate in your environment.

What does your team take over?

Your team runs the application in its own systems. During discovery we identify who will own the workflow after launch. The build and handoff center on what those people need to operate it.

What does it cost to start?
