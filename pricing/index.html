How I scope an AI MVP

For a fixed quote, tell me which workflow you want to change, where its data lives, what access your team can grant, and who will own the application. Those details shape the integration, security review, and handoff. If the main question is whether the idea works on your data, the one-week Idea Sprint gives you a prototype before you commit to a build.

How an AI consulting engagement works

Across the market

  • designpixil checked 88 agencies: 36 publish a price, and 41 of the 49 that state a minimum start at $10,000 or more. The median minimum is $25,000.
